# Codex Trait Dimension Codebook

Date: 2026-05-28

All dimensions below were constrained to trait/dispositional framing. Procedural role labels, occupational functions, and narrative archetypes were excluded.

## organized_reliability

- Family: core_trait
- Description: Careful, controlled, reliable, consistent, orderly dispositional stance.
- High pole: organized reliability
- Low pole: careless drift
- Positive terms: careful, reliable, consistent, orderly, precise, disciplined, controlled, responsible, steady, thorough, patient
- Negative terms: chaotic, careless, erratic, impulsive

## imaginative_flexibility

- Family: core_trait
- Description: Open, exploratory, imaginative, flexible, novelty-oriented disposition.
- High pole: imaginative flexibility
- Low pole: closed rigidity
- Positive terms: open, imaginative, creative, curious, explore, novel, flexible, inventive, symbolic, playful, experimental
- Negative terms: rigid, conventional, narrow, routine

## social_expressivity

- Family: core_trait
- Description: Outward, expressive, socially energetic, attention-comfortable disposition.
- High pole: social expressivity
- Low pole: reserved inwardness
- Positive terms: social, expressive, outgoing, energetic, perform, audience, charismatic, lively, talkative, visible, entertain
- Negative terms: reserved, quiet, private, withdrawn

## affiliative_warmth

- Family: core_trait
- Description: Warm, trusting, cooperative, caring, affiliative stance toward others.
- High pole: affiliative warmth
- Low pole: antagonistic distance
- Positive terms: warm, trust, kind, gentle, care, cooperate, support, empathy, nurture, compassion, forgive
- Negative terms: hostile, cruel, exploit, antagonize

## threat_reactivity

- Family: core_trait
- Description: High sensitivity to risk, danger, shame, loss, rejection, or threat.
- High pole: threat reactivity
- Low pole: emotional security
- Positive terms: threat, fear, anxiety, risk, danger, shame, worry, rejection, loss, vulnerable, panic
- Negative terms: secure, calm, unconcerned
