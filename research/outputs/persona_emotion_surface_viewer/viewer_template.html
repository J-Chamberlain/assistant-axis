How to read this landscape

This is an exploratory overlay of six saved emotion directions on Qwen role-centroid representations. It does not measure emotion frequency or subjective experience. The slider selects categories; the brief transition is a visual morph, not a measured mixture.

Affinities use released role row 47 to match the emotion probes' hidden_states[48] boundary. The horizontal persona PCA is the existing layer-averaged geometry. Emotion probes came from story last tokens, while role centroids summarize response tokens; that transfer remains unvalidated.

The surface uses a regularized thin-plate spline. It is masked outside the role hull and in sparse regions. Density support is not a confidence interval. Vertical units are standardized emotion affinity; their display aspect is chosen for readability and is not commensurate with horizontal PC units.
