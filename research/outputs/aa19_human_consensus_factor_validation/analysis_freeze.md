## Model bridge gate

A bridge reconstruction sets all nonselected trait weights to zero, retains frozen signs and weights, and standardizes the resulting 275 scores for Pearson, Spearman, RMSE, and persona-order tests. The consensus comparator is the AA-18 shared persona score; its bridge predictor is the mean of the three signed model bridge scores. Evaluate (1) all 45 direct labels, (2) direct labels with exact human-source aliases collapsed, (3) 74 direct-plus-close labels, and (4) future-safe labels with every HiFWB-overlapping measure removed and duplicate aliases collapsed. The primary human gate uses unique-source, future-safe direct labels. Collapse exact source groups by a fixed alphabetical representative; an inverse-polarity alias retains that representative's human direction. Report full mapping and the alternative all-label sensitivity. Persona bootstrap: 200 draws, seed 19019. Leave-one-selected-trait-out: all selected traits.

An axis is bridge-adequate only when primary direct-safe reconstruction has Pearson ≥0.75 and Spearman ≥0.70 in **each** of Qwen, Llama, Gemma, and consensus, both signed poles retain at least 10% of their full absolute consensus loading mass, each pole has at least two distinct mapped source groups with |consensus loading|≥0.20, and no one selected source contributes more than half of mapped absolute loading mass. Bootstrap 5th-percentile Pearson must exceed 0.60 for every model and consensus. Inadequate axes are classified D before any human alignment is considered. Model-side signed score checks use training-free frozen AA-18 weights; no refit is allowed.

## Post-run terminology correction (2026-09-17)

The phrase “marginal-preserving reference” above is too broad. The executed and frozen code uses independent Gaussian draws with the observed median effective proxy-pair N; it preserves the unit-variance reference scale, not empirical item marginals or SAPA's joint missingness mask. The parallel-analysis count is therefore an approximate reference and is interpreted alongside MAP, held-out reconstruction, bootstrap, and split halves. No decision rule or result was changed by this wording correction.

## Post-run split-sensitivity audit (2026-09-17)

Quality review found that the initial implementation advanced one random stream through Gaussian parallel analysis before drawing held-out folds, making the folds depend on the number of parallel draws. The implementation now uses separate deterministic streams (`seed+101` and `seed+102`); the original retention and classification thresholds remain intact. Eight independent five-fold split sets and five-versus-six-factor loading projections are reported as **post-freeze sensitivity**, not as a new selection rule. The predeclared primary stream selects five human factors; six is retained as a material alternative when interpreting individual axis claims.
