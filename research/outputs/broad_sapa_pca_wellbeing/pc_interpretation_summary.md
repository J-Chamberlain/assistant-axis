# PC interpretation

PC signs were oriented by the largest predictor loading, never by wellbeing. Parallel analysis supports K=26 under the documented 200-replication run. Split-half results support the K=3 subspace (median canonical correlation 0.940), but individual axes are not promoted as stable psychological dimensions. PC1 remains essentially unrelated to wellbeing (r=-0.004); strongest observed associations are PC4 (r=0.274), PC20 (r=-0.176), PC18 (r=0.138), and PC7 (r=0.137).
