# Geometry-Blind Big Five Mapping Protocol

The mapping stage is deliberately separated from all new geometry analysis. It reads only the 240 canonical trait labels and definitions, the completed SAPA Phase-1b provisional bridge, and the frozen public-domain IPIP/IPIP-NEO domain/facet hierarchy. It does not read persona coordinates, PC rankings, trait-PC associations, model scores, clusters, surfaces, viewer data, AA-4 results, or the motivating screenshot.

`human_anchored_strict_trait_mapping.csv` contains only defensible Big Five assignments among `ACCEPT_DIRECT` rows in `sapa_trait_bridge_provisional_v1.csv`. `human_anchored_extended_trait_mapping.csv` adds defensible `ACCEPT_CLOSE` rows as a separately flagged sensitivity tier; it never silently promotes them to direct evidence. Reviewed SAPA links whose response-style construct was not defensibly assignable to an IPIP-NEO facet remain excluded.

`external_taxonomy_expanded_trait_mapping.csv` audits all 240 canonical traits. Only high-confidence direct facet indicators enter construction C. Medium-confidence proxy/substitute candidates remain visible but are excluded from the composite, and unmapped traits are recorded explicitly. The mapping is invariant across Qwen, Llama, and Gemma.

The external anchor constrains construct selection but does not make the activation-derived scores independent human psychometric measurements. The eventual analysis remains same-space model evidence.
