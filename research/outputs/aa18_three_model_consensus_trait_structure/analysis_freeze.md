# AA-18 analysis freeze

Frozen 2026-09-17 after source/provenance gate and before inspecting substantive trait or component rankings. Inputs are the three AA-17-verified, saved, signed 275 × 240 role–trait cosine matrices. The same 275 persona labels and 240 trait labels appear in the same order. No activation-PC alignment, HiFWB value, SAPA outcome, human respondent, or semantic trait label enters component fitting or selection.

## Preprocessing and direct audit

For within-model analysis, center and divide every trait column by its sample SD across personas; keep signed direction. All fold-fitted means, SDs, SVD bases, and ridge weights are learned on training personas only. Raw cosine means, SDs, ranges, and between-model raw RMSE remain separate diagnostics. For each trait, compute three pairwise Pearson and Spearman correlations, standardized RMSE, and standardized Cronbach consistency alpha (three model blocks treated as raters, not absolute agreement). The frozen all-three convergence score is `max(0, minimum pairwise Pearson) × max(0, mean pairwise Pearson)`; a reversed or weak pair cannot be hidden by a strong pair. Report pairwise-only when best Pearson ≥0.5 and minimum <0.3; all-three strong when minimum ≥0.5. Use 200 persona bootstraps (seed 18018) for 5th/95th percentile intervals and leave-one-persona-out influence. Reciprocal persona profiles are compared across the 240 standardized trait coordinates; all-three strong and pairwise-only use the same cutoffs.

## Primary multiview model

Use regularized MAXVAR generalized CCA: per model, SVD of its training-standardized matrix; retain its top 20 singular directions, form a ridge projector with `s²/(s² + n_train)` shrinkage, and eigendecompose the sum of the three model projectors. The top 20 orthogonal training-persona directions are candidate consensus components. Model-specific trait weights are regularized least-squares projections of each shared direction back onto that model's retained right-singular vectors. This is an equivalent multiblock latent-variable method, not a pooled nominal sample. Scale each model score within training personas; align score signs to the shared training direction. Trait loadings are correlations of the signed standardized trait variables with model scores. A consensus trait loading is the mean of the three signed model loadings only after score alignment; its minimum pairwise loading congruence is always reported.

Use deterministic 5-fold persona-held-out evaluation. In each fold, refit all preprocessing, SVDs, projectors, weights, and score normalization. Evaluate the minimum and mean of three pairwise held-out score correlations separately for each component. The primary regularization is `n_train`; sensitivities use `0.1 n_train` and `10 n_train`, and PCA training ranks 10 and 40 instead of 20. Fold matching, if required, uses training scores only. Component signs and order are not intrinsically meaningful: numerical component order follows projector eigenvalues; full-sample signs are oriented by the largest absolute consensus trait loading.

## Retention and falsification

Use 100 independent persona-correspondence permutations (Qwen fixed, Llama and Gemma independently shuffled) as the primary null for each ordered held-out component. Use 100 trait-label permutations for direct loading/trait-profile alignment nulls. A provisional retained component must be contiguous from component 1, have five-fold pooled held-out minimum pairwise Pearson ≥0.4, all three pairwise values positive, and empirical correspondence-null p≤0.05. Bootstrap and split-half stability can downgrade axis interpretation or the decision. Eigenvalue scree is descriptive. Sensitivity is supportive when a candidate direction or retained subspace matches the primary loading/score subspace above 0.8 after permutation/sign alignment. Do not select a dimension by semantic appeal or bridge coverage.

For leave-one-model-out prediction, derive top 1–5 pairwise projector components from the two source models using training personas only, average their normalized source scores, fit a unit-ridge map to the third model’s training-standardized 240 traits, and report held-out matrix R². Use 100 persona bootstraps and 20 repeated split halves for the retained count. Align axes by maximum absolute score correlation (Hungarian assignment), correct signs, and report axis score/loading congruence and canonical correlations of subspaces. Individually stable means median bootstrap axis score correlation ≥0.85, 10th percentile ≥0.65, and split-half median ≥0.70. A stable subspace requires median minimum canonical correlation ≥0.80. For headline decision A, at least one individually stable component must additionally have held-out all-three minimum score correlation ≥0.50 and minimum pairwise trait-loading Tucker congruence ≥0.70, exceed nulls, and survive sensitivities. B denotes stable common subspace with unresolved axes; C pairwise-only convergence; D reproducible named traits but no stable latent system; E no reliable shared structure.

## Independent joint/individual validation

Use an AJIVE-like principal-angle validation on the three independently fitted top-20 model row subspaces. Eigenvalues of the sum of their orthogonal projectors define candidate joint directions; 100 independent persona-correspondence permutations supply rank-wise 95th-percentile nulls. A joint direction must also have projector eigenvalue above 2 (out of 3), preventing a strong pair alone from defining the all-three joint rank. Decompose each standardized matrix by orthogonal projection onto retained joint directions, then top `20 − joint_rank` PCA directions of its residual; remaining variance is residual. This is an operational joint/individual variance partition, not a uniquely identified causal decomposition. On joint-residual matrices, evaluate pairwise model projector intersections against the same correspondence null; report pairwise ranks where estimable. Compare AJIVE-like joint scores to GCCA shared scores by canonical correlation, without using AJIVE ranks to select the primary components.

## Post-fit comparison and human bridge

Compare frozen AA-17 pattern loadings and persona factor scores to AA-18 components by Tucker congruence, score correlations, subspace canonical correlations, and variance overlap. Compare each AA-17 factor with the joint projection and model-specific residual projection, not verbal labels alone. Only after all numerical decisions are frozen, overlay the existing 45 direct/74 total AA-16 semantic bridge labels, HiFWB item-overlap flags, exact source groups, and duplicated source items. Per component report counts, absolute loading mass captured, positive/negative pole balance, and correlation of the full score with a score reconstructed from mapped traits using frozen loadings. The latter is descriptive and uses no human outcomes. No SAPA/HiFWB data enter the model.
