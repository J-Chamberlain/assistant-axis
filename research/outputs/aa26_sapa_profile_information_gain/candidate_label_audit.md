# Item-content naming audit

The immutable pre-performance measurement freeze used the working label “Stress reactivity” for PS:PS:S. Review of its 14 retained existing item wordings shows broader behavioral dysregulation, repeated trouble, doubts, and emotional instability. The reporting label is therefore corrected to **Behavioral and emotional self-regulation (PS:S subset)**. No item membership, key, eligibility, grouping, score, selection, or prediction changed. The frozen label remains visible for provenance; analysis/report rendering uses the corrected display label.

Coping under pressure is the NEOn6 vulnerability subset: higher source-keyed values indicate more vulnerability, not better coping. Cheerfulness uses the frozen NEOe6 key exactly. Two counterintuitive source-key examples remain explicit: q_1567 “Radiate joy.” is reverse-keyed in NEOe6, and q_824 “Feel desperate.” is positively keyed in PSs. These signs are inherited from the documented superKey696 source, not invented or silently corrected here. They warrant source-key/content review before constructing any future elicitation measure. Official-key agreement is an implementation gate, not independent validation of every source key or partial scale. All exact item signs are in selected_question_or_construct_set.csv; wordings are in repair/candidate_existing_questions.csv.
