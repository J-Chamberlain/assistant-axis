    Scientific scope and method

    Trait values are activation-derived, same-space Qwen/Qwen3-32B trait affinities. Percentiles are relative to the 275 canonical Qwen personas and are converted back to raw activation-cosine values with the predictor CLI’s empirical interpolation and clamping rule.

    The displayed point is generated by the validated raw-cosine Ridge mapping. For an existing selected persona, its Ridge bundle was trained and tuned without that persona. An edited profile therefore yields a predicted counterfactual location, not observed model behavior.

    Trait edits are not causal interventions, and this viewer does not show human personality measurement. OOD labels are heuristic geometric diagnostics, not calibrated probabilities.
