## Frozen match statuses

- `STRONG_FAMILY_MATCH`: the documented construct captures the central shared content of the filtered marker family, including its main anchors, with limited important mismatch.
- `PARTIAL_FAMILY_MATCH`: the construct captures a substantial, coherent part of the family but leaves important central content uncovered.
- `FACET_OR_SUBCOMPONENT`: the construct measures a narrower component of a compound or heterogeneous family.
- `ITEM_LEVEL_ONLY`: no adequate documented scale is available, but one or more exact SAPA items supply relevant narrow content.
- `POOR_MATCH`: a candidate considered but judged too indirect or mismatched; retained only when necessary to document a tempting false match.
- `NO_DEFENSIBLE_MATCH`: no available SAPA construct/item counterpart is defensible, including mechanically empty marker families.

Every retained proposal must identify covered and uncovered markers, measurement direction, construct/item provenance, and overlap with other proposals. A strong label may not be carried forward merely because it was strong for the prior, broader association family.

## Boundaries

These are semantic measurement-availability judgments, not human/model equivalence or psychometric validation. No respondent is scored or projected, and no downstream geometry or cross-model result is used.
