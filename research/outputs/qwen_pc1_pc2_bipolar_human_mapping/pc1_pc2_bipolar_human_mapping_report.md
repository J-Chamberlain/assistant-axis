## Revised interpretations

PC1 is best described as **externally legible, standards-governed empirical/procedural discipline versus internally generated expressive-symbolic possibility**. The positive side combines factual transparency, analysis, method, and accountable correct execution. The negative side combines narrative, poetic, metaphorical, aesthetic, mystical, affective, and improvisational expression. Broad Conscientiousness captures only part of the positive mechanism and part of the behavioral inverse. It should be decomposed rather than retained as the axis label.

PC2 is best described as **direct, situated experiential engagement versus inward, abstract, formally mediated integration**. Its positive side combines experience, practical application, inquiry, accessibility, and some developmental/social immediacy. Its negative side combines introversion, reflection, theory, abstraction, conceptual integration, ritual, reverence, principle, and solemn form. Embodied physical engagement is a modest secondary positive-PC2 ingredient, not the principal axis meaning.

No single SAPA construct captures either whole axis. PC1 requires a positive standards/execution hierarchy and a negative imaginative-expression hierarchy. PC2 requires inverse Intellect plus distinct social-outward, activity/experience, developmental-immediacy, and ritual/formal components. These are proposed measurement analogues, not human/model correspondence.

### B. Best whole-axis interpretation

The smallest hierarchy that explains both poles is:

1. **Operating mode:** externally constrained and legible versus internally generative and interpretive.
2. **Positive mechanisms:** empirical-analytic epistemic practice plus procedural correctness, accountability, and disciplined execution.
3. **Negative mechanisms:** expressive-symbolic imagination plus rhetorical, affective, aesthetic, and improvisational possibility.

This is broader than Conscientiousness and narrower than generic expertise. It describes a model operating mode that couples how claims are warranted with how responses are stylistically generated.

### C. Remaining competing interpretations

- **Broad Conscientiousness:** real but incomplete. It explains method, caution, order, and some spontaneity/impulsivity inverse, not factual transparency or the symbolic negative family.
- **Intellect/expertise:** real but incomplete. It explains analysis and disciplined knowledge, but human Intellect emphasizes abstract ideas that also characterize negative PC2.
- **Openness/imagination:** strong for negative PC1, weak as a positive-pole inverse.
- **Institutional/professional legibility:** well supported by role extremes, but partly confounded with role prompt conventions and cluster composition.
- **Sincerity/honesty:** a narrow transparency analogue. It would incorrectly moralize the expressive-symbolic pole if used as the axis label.

### D-E. Human construct match and hierarchy

No single established construct captures PC1. The best available hierarchy is:

- Positive standards/execution domain: Dutifulness is the clearest external-obligation analogue. Orderliness, Perfectionism, Cautiousness, Prudence, and Control cover structure and error control. Intellect covers analytic reasoning. Honesty/Propriety and Sincerity cover limited rule-respect and nondeception content.
- Negative imagination/expression domain: the BFAS Openness aspect is the strongest negative-pole analogue. Imagination, Artistic Interests, Absorption, and Expressiveness cover narrower subfamilies.

Broad Conscientiousness is retained only as a partial component and should be decomposed. Self-Discipline and Industriousness are downgraded because prior model-side evidence favors accountability over generic effort or determination. Broad Openness/Intellect is also unsuitable as one direction because its Intellect and imaginative facets relate to different model-side structures.

### F. Important unmeasured Qwen content

SAPA has no single construct for disciplined empirical transparency, evidence-grounded communication, externally legible correct execution, or their opposition to symbolic/rhetorical generativity. The rule and procedure item audit finds 39 lexical candidates, including explicit rules, duties, mistakes, standards, details, authority, planning, and order. These remain item-level evidence. No individual item is treated as a validated construct.

### G. Retain, downgrade, or replace

- Retain Openness, Imagination, Artistic Interests, Absorption, and Expressiveness for the negative pole.
- Retain Orderliness, Perfectionism, Cautiousness, Intellect, and Sincerity as bounded positive facets.
- Add Dutifulness and Honesty/Propriety as explicit external-rule components.
- Downgrade broad Conscientiousness from axis label to partial domain and decompose it.
- Downgrade Self-Discipline, Industriousness, Achievement Striving, and Traditionalism as primary explanations.
- Reject Self-Efficacy as a meaningful axis analogue.

### H. Evidence that would discriminate remaining interpretations

Discriminating evidence would need to separate external standards, empirical rigor, professional legibility, and expressive register while keeping the others stable. It would also need to show whether the same bipolar ordering holds outside professional and mythic role clusters. This report does not design or run that test.

### B. Best whole-axis interpretation

The best hierarchy is:

1. **Primary mode of engagement:** direct, situated, experiential contact versus abstract, conceptual, and mediated integration.
2. **Positive expressions:** practical application, accessibility, social outwardness, developmental/reactive immediacy, and a modest embodied/physical component.
3. **Negative expressions:** inward reflection, theory, conceptual world-modeling, ritual/formal mediation, reverence, principle, and solemn erudition.

The label keeps `experiential` broader than physicality. Direct experience may be bodily, practical, social, or developmental. The opposite pole is not mere inactivity. It is structured distance from immediate experience through concepts, reflection, ritual, and formal mediation.

### C. Remaining competing interpretations

- **Practical/concrete versus abstract/theoretical:** the cleanest marker-level opposition, but new practical role coding is weak and does not explain developmental/social extremes.
- **Extraversion versus Introversion:** logically coherent across `introverted` versus broader `extroverted` and `gregarious`, but social-outward role coding is weak.
- **Embodied versus disembodied:** modest independent evidence, substantial counterexamples, and no direct human scale.
- **Developmental/reactive immediacy versus mature integration:** strong role-extreme context, but not a validated human developmental measure.
- **Ritual/formal mediation versus spontaneity/flexibility:** a real negative-pole component, but interpersonal Flexibility is not the correct opposite.
- **Coherent action under unresolved uncertainty:** retained only as a secondary expression. Abstraction is stronger, uncertainty exposure previously failed, and the professional correlation was near zero.

### D-E. Human construct match and hierarchy

No single construct captures PC2. The best available hierarchy is:

- Abstract/integrative negative pole: Intellect is the leading established analogue, with a proposed higher-score direction toward negative PC2. Traditionalism and Seriousness cover ritual/conventional and solemn-form facets.
- Social bipolar component: Extraversion is retained and strengthened as an explicit opposite-pole component because `introverted` is the strongest strict negative marker and `extroverted`/`gregarious` are broader positive target-dominant markers. It remains only partial because social-outward role ratings correlate weakly with PC2.
- Immediate/experiential positive components: Activity Level covers energy and tempo; Adventurousness covers novelty and new experience; Inquisitiveness matches one strict positive label; Impulsivity covers developmental immediacy. None measures the whole positive pole.
- Embodiment: no direct SAPA construct is available.

Broad Openness/Intellect and Plasticity should be decomposed because they combine ingredients that point toward both PC2 poles. Human Inquisitiveness is especially ambiguous: it literally matches the positive marker, but its knowledge-oriented items can also cohere with the negative abstract pole.

### F. Important unmeasured Qwen content

The validated 126-construct library has no direct athleticism, physical-activity, bodily-engagement, manual-practice, or hands-on-experience construct. The 194-item audit spanning Extraversion, Activity Level, adventure/risk, fear, inquiry, and related candidates found semantic neighbors, not a defensible physicality scale. Activity Level measures energy, busyness, and pace. Adventurousness measures novelty and variety. Excitement Seeking and Harm Avoidance measure risk. Fearfulness measures danger sensitivity. None should be renamed physicality.

The library also lacks a single construct opposing practical/direct experience to ritualized, conceptual, integrated world-modeling.

### G. Retain, downgrade, or replace

- Retain Intellect as the leading but incomplete negative-pole analogue.
- Retain Extraversion as an explicit bipolar social component, not a whole-axis match; add Gregariousness as a narrow facet.
- Retain Traditionalism as a ritual/conventional component and add Seriousness as a narrow register facet.
- Retain Inquisitiveness only as a marker-level analogue with a bipolar contradiction.
- Retain Adventurousness but downgrade it as evidence for embodiment.
- Add Activity Level only as an energy/tempo neighbor.
- Retain Impulsivity as a secondary developmental-immediacy component.
- Downgrade Flexibility, Excitement Seeking, Absorption, and Control as primary explanations.
- Replace broad Openness/Intellect and Plasticity with decomposed facets.
- Reject Harm Avoidance and Fearfulness as physicality measures.

### H. Evidence that would discriminate remaining interpretations

Discriminating evidence would need separate measures of direct experience, practical application, social outwardness, bodily engagement, abstract conceptualization, developmental integration, and ritual versus institutional formality on the same blinded material. It would also need to show whether physicality persists after these dimensions and cluster structure are separated. This report does not select or execute that work.

## Epistemic status and stopping point

Observed evidence includes frozen specificity memberships and metrics, role coordinates and instructions, prior completed Qwen-only diagnostics, the 126-construct library, the 696-item dictionary, and the new coordinate-blind role codes. The axis descriptions and SAPA mappings are interpretations. A shared latent property between a human construct and a Qwen PC remains a hypothesis. Human respondent correspondence, causal mechanism, behavioral realization, and cross-model generalization remain unknown.

This is the requested stopping point. No specificity analysis was rerun or retuned. No human respondent was scored or projected. No human/model correspondence test was run. No Llama/Gemma comparison or AA-7 result was used to choose interpretations. No next experiment was selected.
