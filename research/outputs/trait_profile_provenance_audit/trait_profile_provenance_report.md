## Concise Verdict

The trait matrix is **mixed**.

Observed: the 240 trait vocabulary, trait prompt artifacts, and Qwen trait vectors are inherited from the Assistant Axis / Lu et al. artifact family, with local copies aligned to the Hugging Face vector and prompt releases. Observed: the 275-role x 240-trait CSV matrix itself was generated inside this project by deterministic linear algebra over the released Qwen role and trait activation vectors.

Methodological implication: the matrix is best treated as an activation-space role-to-trait cosine profile, not as an independent psychological rating table. It is useful for explaining and visualizing Qwen persona geometry, but it is not evidentially independent from the Qwen role-vector PCA geometry because both sides share the same released Qwen activation-vector source.

### 7. How were trait scores assigned to each role?

Observed: scores were assigned by cosine similarity between mean-pooled, L2-normalized Qwen role vectors and mean-pooled, L2-normalized Qwen trait vectors.

Observed in `run_trait_persona_prediction.py`:

- `load_mean_vector(path)` loads a tensor with `torch.load(...)`.
- If the tensor is multidimensional, it computes `tensor.mean(0)`.
- It converts to NumPy and divides by the L2 norm.
- The matrix is computed as `similarity = role_vectors @ trait_vectors.T`.
- The output is saved as `persona_trait_similarity_matrix.csv`.

### 8. Were scores assigned from bare role names, role descriptions, system prompts, generated role responses, activation vectors, or another artifact?

### 9. What model or scoring procedure produced the scores?

Observed scoring procedure: deterministic cosine similarity over released Qwen activation vectors.

Observed vector model: `Qwen/Qwen3-32B`.

Observed local procedure:

1. Load 275 Qwen role tensors from `downloads/hf_vectors/qwen-3-32b/role_vectors/`.
2. Load 240 Qwen trait tensors from `downloads/hf_vectors/qwen-3-32b/trait_vectors/`.
3. Mean-pool each `[64, 5120]` tensor to one 5120-dimensional vector.
4. L2-normalize each mean vector.
5. Compute the 275 x 240 matrix by dot product.

### 11. What level of evidential independence does this matrix have from the role-vector PCA geometry?

Observed: low independence from the role-vector PCA geometry.

Reason:

- The canonical Qwen PCA geometry is based on Qwen role activation vectors.
- The trait-profile matrix rows are the same Qwen role activation vectors projected by cosine onto Qwen trait activation vectors.
- The 240 trait columns are therefore same-model, same-activation-space features.

Inferred: strong reconstruction of role PCA coordinates from this matrix should be interpreted as same-space basis coverage or activation-space alignment, not as independent evidence that psychological trait ratings externally validate the PCA axes.

## Methodological Implication

The trait-enrichment visualizations should be described as activation-space trait-cosine overlays. They show which released Qwen trait vectors are close to roles or PC1/PC2 regions in the same activation-vector space. They do not show independent human-rated trait labels, independent LLM questionnaire ratings, or causal trait mechanisms.

For Paper 1.5 language, the safe formulation is:

- "High-PC2 cells are enriched for Qwen trait vectors such as `experiential`, `reactive`, and `grounded` relative to local PC1-band baselines."

Avoid:

- "High-PC2 roles are objectively more experiential/reactive/grounded."
- "Trait ratings independently validate PC2."
- "The trait matrix proves a psychological ontology."

The matrix remains useful because it provides an internally consistent vocabulary for inspecting activation-space neighborhoods. Its limitation is that it is not independent of the geometry it helps explain.
