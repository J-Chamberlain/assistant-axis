# LISS modifiable-pathway research program

## Long-term question
Given a person’s baseline trait profile, current wellbeing profile, behavior, and context, which feasible changes in modifiable behavior or context predict the largest subsequent improvement in particular wellbeing domains?

`W_(t+1) = f(W_t, T_t, B_t, C_t, T_t × B_t, B_t × C_t)`

Here, W is wellbeing, T is relatively stable disposition, B is modifiable behavior/state, and C is context/constraint. This equation defines a staged research program; it does not assert that every interaction is identifiable or powered.

## Three distinct targets

1. **Trait advantage:** baseline profiles correlate with different average wellbeing.
2. **Behavioral compensation:** a behavior may especially benefit people with a characteristic vulnerability.
3. **Strength-congruent pathways:** different profiles may reach similar wellbeing through behaviors compatible with their dispositions.

## Stages

1. Validate HiFWB measurement and ordinary personality associations in the 2020 cross-section.
2. Harmonize repeated personality, wellbeing, behavior, and context variables; quantify overlap and attrition.
3. Separate stable between-person differences from within-person deviations.
4. Test a small prespecified set of behavior/context main effects and trait × behavior interactions using held-out evaluation.
5. Add causal-strengthening designs only where timing, positivity, confounder measurement, and consistency assumptions are credible.
6. Replicate across LISS, UAS, and GFS; use SAPA for static psychometric structure.

No individualized recommendation or causal optimization result exists at this stage.
