# LISS 2020 measurement notes

Study 965 (Personality, May–June 2020; DOI 10.17026/dans-z2c-fzcd) reports 6,969 selected, 5,923 responses, and 5,859 completes. Expected files are `cp20l_EN_1.0p.dta` and `codebook_cp20l_EN_1.1.pdf`; the join key is `nomem_encr`. Focal blocks include happiness/life satisfaction (`cp20l010–011`), SWLS (`014–018`), 50-item IPIP Big Five (`020–069`), Rosenberg self-esteem (`070–079`), IOS (`135`), PANAS (`146–165`), and LOT-R (`198–207`).

Study 1105 (Social Science, May 2020; DOI 10.17026/dans-25d-n6kr) reports 3,571 selected, 2,724 responses, and 2,719 completes. Expected files are `ss20a_EN_1.0p.dta` and `codebook_ss20a_EN_1.0.pdf`; join key `nomem_encr`. `ss20a001` assigns four versions: version 1 receives the revised 18-item past-week form (`002–019`) and original 6-item past-month form (`020–025`); version 2 receives original 14 items past month (`026–039`); version 3 receives original 14 items past week (`040–053`); version 4 receives revised 18 items past month (`054–071`). Responses are 0–5 frequency categories. Preserve form and timeframe; test equivalence before pooling.

Legacy MHC-SF factors are comparison structures, not HiFWB lenses. Emotional wellbeing overlaps Subjective wellbeing. Psychological wellbeing redistributes across Meaning-making and Self-concept and includes autonomy outside the example core. Social wellbeing includes relevant belonging/contribution and excluded societal acceptance/coherence/actualization. The codebook's `nomem_encr2` passage is treated as a typo because the documented merge key is `nomem_encr`.
