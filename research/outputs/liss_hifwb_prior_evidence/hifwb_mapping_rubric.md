# HiFWB mapping rubric (AA-11 repair)

This is a provisional analytic mapping rubric, not an official HiFWB scoring key.

- **DIRECT:** source wording directly represents the proposed characteristic.
- **CLOSE:** defensible adjacent or inverse evidence, retained separately.
- **PARTIAL:** plausible but ambiguous or only embedded in a broader construct.
- **NON_CORE_EXCLUDED:** retained for audit but excluded from the example HiFWB core.
- **UNKNOWN:** source evidence is insufficient to judge.

MHC-SF Emotional wellbeing substantially overlaps Subjective wellbeing. Its Psychological factor mixes characteristics that HiFWB redistributes: meaning/direction and personal growth map to Meaning-making; self-acceptance and mastery map to Self-concept; autonomy/independent expression is excluded from the example core. Its Social factor mixes relevant belonging/contribution with social acceptance, coherence, and actualization, which are excluded. Recent-frequency happiness maps to Affect/positive affect. Interested in life remains PARTIAL.

Questionnaire coverage and prior personality-association evidence are recorded separately. An available item is not evidence that a prior paper isolated or tested the corresponding HiFWB node.
