# HiFWB mapping rubric (AA-11)

This rubric is an analytic mapping aid, not an official HiFWB scoring key.

- **DIRECT:** item wording directly represents the named characteristic and its lens.
- **CLOSE:** defensible adjacent representation; retain as secondary/sensitivity evidence.
- **PARTIAL/AMBIGUOUS:** plausible but insufficiently specific; do not use as a core score without a later decision.
- **NON_CORE_EXCLUDED:** retain for audit transparency but do not treat as core HiFWB evidence.

Map multivariate behavioral signatures, not one-to-one trait names. Do not force traditional MHC-SF emotional/psychological/social subscales into HiFWB lenses. The shared human/model feature space remains deliberately unresolved in AA-11.
