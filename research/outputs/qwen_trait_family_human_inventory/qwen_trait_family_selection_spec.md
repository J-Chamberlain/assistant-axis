# Qwen trait-family selection specification

Status: frozen before family description or SAPA matching  
Scope: Qwen/Qwen3-32B only; 275 saved roles; 240 activation-derived trait-affinity features; PC1-PC6  
Primary threshold: absolute Pearson correlation of at least 0.50

## Association calculation

For each Qwen PC and each of the 240 canonical Qwen trait-affinity columns, calculate Pearson correlation across the same 275 saved roles. Also retain Pearson r-squared, the two-sided Pearson p-value, Spearman rho, absolute-rank within the PC, and rank within the correlation pole. Roles are joined by canonical persona name; row order is never assumed.

Benjamini-Hochberg false-discovery-rate adjustment is performed separately within each PC's 240 Pearson tests. The implementation uses the standard reverse cumulative minimum of `p * m / rank`, clipped to 1.0.

## Bootstrap sign stability

Use 2,000 nonparametric role-bootstrap resamples per PC. Each resample draws 275 role indices with replacement and applies the same sampled indices to the PC score and all 240 trait columns. Randomness is generated by NumPy PCG64 with master seed `20260912` and deterministic PC-specific seeds `20260913` through `20260918` for PC1 through PC6. The calculation is performed in fixed batches without changing the random draw sequence.

For a nonzero observed correlation, sign stability is the fraction of finite bootstrap correlations with the same sign as the observed correlation. Nonfinite bootstrap correlations, if any, are recorded and excluded from the denominator; any trait with no finite bootstrap estimates fails the stability rule.

## Frozen family rule

A trait is a PRIMARY family member for a PC when all three conditions hold:

1. `abs(Pearson r) >= 0.50`.
2. `BH-FDR q < 0.01` within that PC's 240 tests.
3. Bootstrap correlation sign matches the observed sign in at least 95% of valid resamples.

Positive and negative correlations form separate families. If a PC/pole has no member, it remains empty; no threshold is relaxed.

Sensitivity membership uses the identical FDR and sign-stability rules at absolute-r thresholds 0.40 and 0.60. The 0.50 rule remains primary regardless of the results.

## Interpretation firewall

Membership is numerical and is frozen before any family description or SAPA matching. Family selection does not use role labels, prior PC interpretation labels, Big Five projections, prior AA-1 human-construct hypotheses, AA-7 outputs, Llama, or Gemma. The SAPA mapping stage receives only neutral family IDs, selected Qwen trait names and canonical definitions, correlation strengths, anchors, and descriptions derived from the selected members.

## Epistemic boundary

The correlations and threshold membership are observed same-space Qwen activation-artifact relationships. A semantic description of a selected family or a proposed SAPA counterpart is interpretation. Whether any Qwen family and human construct reflect a shared latent property remains a hypothesis; human/model quantitative correspondence is unknown.
