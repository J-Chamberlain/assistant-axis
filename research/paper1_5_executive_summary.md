# Paper 1.5 Executive Summary

## Working Title

Interpreting Persona Activation Geometry

## Summary

Paper 1.5 studies what structure is present in persona activation geometry after the role corpus and elicitation method have been stress tested. It no longer needs to be framed primarily as an adaptive extraction replication paper. The Qwen/Qwen3-32B trickster extraction remains important because it demonstrates the tooling path: detached H100/A100 execution, response preservation, activation-shard preservation, local integrity, role-expression scoring, and vector validation. The scientific claim now lives in the layered interpretation of geometry.

The preliminary due diligence shows that the elicitation corpus is not neutral. Role prompts frequently expose the persona label, no-label prompt ablations preserve much of the prompt-space semantic topology, and semantic prompt geometry only partially predicts activation-space clustering. This motivates treating the Lu et al. role inventory as a constructed semantic role manifold whose structure is transformed by model activation geometry rather than as a transparent sample of human psychological kinds.

The current best interpretation is layered. Semantic structure explains some topology. Big Five-style trait features explain broad global placement. Procedural and operating-mode features add a smaller local residual correction. TF-IDF/SVD lexical-register features explain additional residual variance, especially concrete prompt texture that abstract hand-named dimensions flatten. Developmental, liminal, collective, and other residual cases remain difficult and should be treated as diagnostic regions rather than solved ontology.

The mechanistic interpretability contribution is a method for decomposing persona geometry into semantic, dispositional, procedural, lexical/register, and residual layers. This reframes persona clusters as interpretable representational structure while preserving caution about prompt artifacts, evaluator effects, and cultural coverage. The paper should not claim that the clusters are a true psychology of persons.

Future H100 work belongs to Paper 2 and grant-supported follow-up, not to the Paper 1.5 prerequisite path. The next compute-intensive frontier is local centroid perturbation around selected anchors such as Trickster, Actor, Therapist, and Spy, testing whether persona space has reusable local directions or is strongly curved and anchor-dependent.
