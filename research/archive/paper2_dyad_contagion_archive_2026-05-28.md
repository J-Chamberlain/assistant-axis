# Archived Paper 2 Dyad and Contagion Framing

## Archive Status

This note archives the earlier Paper 2 framing as of 2026-05-28. The ideas are not discarded. They are no longer the active Paper 2 scope, which is now local centroid perturbation and local persona-manifold mapping.

## Archived Scope

The earlier Paper 2 plan centered on dyadic persona contagion, anchored interviewers, attractor collapse, conversational drift, rumination dynamics, leakage control, and cap-stabilized interviewer personas. This work produced useful operational lessons, including the KV-cache measurement artifact, the need for `use_cache=False` during measurement forward passes, the importance of non-leaking prompts, and the distinction between surface behavioral imitation and internal geometric drift.

## Why It Is Archived

The current Paper 1.5 program has shifted from extraction replication toward layered geometry interpretation. The most direct next experimental step is therefore local mapping around persona centroids, not broad dyadic dynamics. Local centroid perturbation tests the structure that Paper 1.5 now claims exists: semantic, dispositional, procedural, lexical/register, and residual layers organized in activation space.

## Future Use

The dyad and attractor-collapse material remains relevant for later work on conversational dynamics, persona contagion, computational rumination, and multi-agent interaction. It should be treated as an archived research track rather than as failed or obsolete work.
