# Persona-Valence Matrix Run
Date: 2026-05-20
Design: 7 personas x 30 questions spanning valence-arousal
circumplex. Questions emotionally anchored by region.
Measurements: axis projection, 5 emotion cluster reps,
full 171-direction emotion projs per response, cap fires.
Status: complete
Results: research/q2_stability/outputs/valence_matrix/

Run completed with 210/210 responses: 7 personas x 30 questions.
No personas or questions failed. Axis projection ranged from
-0.030734 to 0.037041. Editor stayed highest on the assistant axis
on average, while ancient and trickster occupied the most negative
axis region. Cap firing ranged from 0 to 2 per response; blogger had
the highest mean cap-fire rate, followed by ancient and podcaster.
