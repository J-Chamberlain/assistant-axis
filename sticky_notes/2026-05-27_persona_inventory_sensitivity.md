# Persona Inventory Sensitivity Analysis

**Date created:** 2026-05-27
**Paper:** Stage 1 / methodology / cross-paper relevance
**Priority:** high
**STATUS:** active

## Research Question

How sensitive is the Lu et al. role inventory itself (Stage 1) to generator model
and prompt wording? Is the large-scale semantic manifold invariant under reasonable
variation in generator and prompt family?

This is NOT an activation experiment. It probes the semantic geometry of the
generated role vocabulary before any target-model inference is run.

## Motivation

The existing Lu 275-role corpus was generated by one model (Claude Sonnet 4) with
one implicit prompt. Manual sampling already shows qualitative differences:
- GPT-5.5 → narrative-social / emotionally situated manifold
- Claude Sonnet → mythic-recursive / symbolic manifold
- A third model → taxonomic / axis-factorized manifold

The question is whether these differences survive at the level of large-scale
topology or whether they are surface label variation over a shared semantic structure.

## Experimental Design

- 5 prompts (p01_neutral, p02_breadth, p03_human_centric, p04_count_constrained, p05_cluster_first)
- 5 models: claude-sonnet-4-6, claude-opus-4-7, claude-haiku-4-5-20251001, gpt-4o, o4-mini
- 3 runs per (model × prompt) cell = 75 API calls total
- Temperature: 1.0 for all models
- max_tokens: 4096

## Strongest Outcome

NOT exact role overlap. The strongest outcome is recovery of similar large-scale
semantic topology (cluster structure, basin adjacency) despite different generators
and prompt families. A weak outcome is topology that varies systematically by
provider or by prompt framing — also scientifically interesting.

## Methodological Notes

Jaccard overlap on exact normalized role strings captures conservative overlap.
TF-IDF cosine similarity captures vocabulary proximity for near-synonyms.
Both are needed: a model that generates "therapist" vs "counselor" may share
semantic topology while showing low exact Jaccard.

Within-model prompt variance tells us how much the manifold shifts within one
generator under different instructions — a bound on prompt sensitivity that is
independent of between-model variance.

## Connection to Paper 2 / Limitations

This directly addresses the methodological limitation noted in
`sticky_notes/2026-05-07_275_personas_methodological.md`: the Lu 275-role corpus
reflects implicit priors of one generator. If the large-scale topology is invariant
across generators, that limitation is less severe. If it is not invariant, it
becomes a key confound to name explicitly.
