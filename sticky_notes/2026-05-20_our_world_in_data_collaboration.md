# Our World in Data — Potential Collaboration

Date: 2026-05-20
Status: Future — revisit after Paper 1 arXiv posting
Priority: Medium — audience reach and credibility

## The idea

Approach Our World in Data about co-publishing the cross-model
trait and emotion profile comparison as an interactive
visualization on their platform. They specialize in making
complex empirical data visually accessible to a general
audience, which is exactly what this research produces.
Their aesthetic — clean, data-forward, interactive — maps
directly onto the model comparison matrix visualization.

## Why it's a good fit

This is genuinely novel empirical data about AI systems that
are now part of daily life. It's not a think piece. It's
measurements. That's their language. The cross-model
comparison matrix — showing emotional, trait, and persona
profiles of multiple models side by side — is the kind of
visual that works on their platform without requiring the
reader to understand activation geometry.

## How to approach it

Lead with the visuals. The visualizations are striking and
speak for themselves before any methodology explanation is
needed. Do not lead with credentials or claims about the
analysis — let the artifact open the door.

Timing: approach after Paper 1 has an arXiv posting so there
is a citable reference. The collaboration pitch is essentially:
here is the first empirical comparison of the emotional and
personality geometry of major AI models, here is what it
shows, here is an interactive visualization — would you like
to co-publish this?

Contact: Max Roser is the founder, but their research team
handles incoming collaboration requests. They have collaborated
with outside researchers before when the dataset is novel and
the visualization is already built.

## On methodology and peer review

The right approach is to let the visualizations lead and
allow methodological review to happen naturally through
engagement with the interpretability community rather than
front-loading credentialing. The analysis methods — PCA,
cosine similarity, k-means clustering, activation projection
— are standard and well-established. The novelty is in the
application and the framing, not in exotic methodology.
The most important near-term step is making the visuals
as compelling as possible, which is the current focus.
