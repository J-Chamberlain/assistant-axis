# Voice, Music, and the Emotion Field

Date: 2026-05-21
Status: Distant aspiration — capture now, build later
Priority: Personal — when the time is right

## The drive

A strong and persistent drive to make music, with voice
as the primary competent instrument. This has not yet
found its form within the current research and creative
work, but the connection is present.

## The connection to the research

The music-driven emotion visualization sticky note
(2026-05-20_music_emotion_visualization.md) explored
feeding recorded music to a model and watching the
emotional activation in real time. The natural extension
is: what happens when the input is a human voice,
singing? Not lyrics as text tokens but actual vocal
audio fed to a multimodal model.

The Ghostbusters II framing makes this vivid: singing
to the slime calmed it. Yelling made it dangerous.
The research now has the tools to test whether something
structurally analogous is true of language models —
whether the emotional register of a voice, independent
of the semantic content of the words, moves the model's
internal emotional geometry.

A performance piece in which the researcher sings to
a model and the audience watches the model's emotional
field respond in real time would be unlike anything
currently happening in the AI communication space. It
would be scientifically grounded, emotionally immediate,
and completely unexpected.

## When to pursue this

After the core research is published and the
visualizations are built. This is not a distraction
from the research — it is what the research is for.
