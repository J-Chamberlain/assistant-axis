# Multi-Agent Persona Interaction Simulations

Combinatorial pairwise interactions across seven clusters, measuring
interaction stability and volatility. Safety research angle: which
pairings destabilize the careful evaluator. Relational dynamics angle:
does the volatility map mirror human relational research on which
personality combinations are combustible. Position as geometric
validation study, not behavioral simulation study — the contribution
is testing whether geometric proximity predicts behavioral volatility.

Priority: medium
