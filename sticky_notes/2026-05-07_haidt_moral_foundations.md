# Haidt Moral Foundations as Empirical Test

Feed Haidt's interview descriptions and character profiles to LLMs,
have them embody archetypes without knowing the source, measure
empirical consistency with Haidt's documented behavioral signatures.
Conservative/liberal archetype tolerance for ambiguity and disorder
(the empty TV channel) as a concrete, non-obvious test case.
Second-order question: where do Haidt's moral foundations map in
the cluster geometry?
